Training programs

Find Your Next Level.

Build fundamentals, repeat the right drills and grow confidence in every part of your game. Ask the academy which options are currently available for your age and skill level.

01 / BAT
🏏

Batting Development

Work on stance, grip, balance, footwork, timing, shot selection and responding to different lines and lengths.

02 / BOWL
🎯

Bowling Skills

Practise run-up rhythm, release point, line and length, accuracy, control and pace or spin fundamentals.

03 / FIELD
🧤

Fielding & Catching

Develop ground fielding, catching technique, throwing accuracy, movement and quick reactions.

04 / START
🌱

Beginner Foundations

Learn the rules, equipment basics and simple batting, bowling and fielding drills step by step.

05 / GAME
📋

Match Awareness

Understand field placements, running between wickets, communication and game situations.

06 / MOVE
⚡

Agility & Fitness

Support cricket performance with movement, coordination, warm-ups, balance and suitable conditioning.
